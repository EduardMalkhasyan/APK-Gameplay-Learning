# Context for this monetization teardown

Copy to `~/Desktop/apk-work/<run-id>/CONTEXT.md` and fill it in. It stays in the work folder and is never committed: it will hold company facts. Agents read it before anything else, and a short, accurate version beats a long one.

Purpose: learn HOW top games monetize so we can design our own improvements. Learning only. Nobody copies code, assets, names or numbers into a product.

## Who asked and why

Who needs this, for what decision, by when. Link the meeting or thread.

## Questions that matter most

The three to five questions this run must answer. Terms or nicknames people use for products, so agents read notes correctly.

## Our games

For each: package id, current ad setup (mediation, floors, pacing values, placements and their traffic share), purchases, key metrics and what is being tested right now that must not be disturbed. Mark games that are ours, so their reports read as audits with action plans.

## Our benchmark

What our own stack already does, so nothing we ship is reported as a gap, and what we know we lack.

## Already known

Earlier teardowns and their conclusions, with links. Agents must not present these as new, and should flag what differs.

## Audiences for the lessons

Who the section 14 lessons are for (for example one game, another game, an SDK roadmap with its current items).

## Evidence rules

Tags: **[data]** bundled config or asset value, **[code]** recovered body or disassembly, **[names]** identifiers only, **[store]** public listing, **[internal]** company sources, **[inferred]** reasoning. A name never proves behaviour. A config key shows a capability, not that it is on. Bundled defaults may be overridden remotely. Say what was not done, for example that no game was run and no traffic was captured.
